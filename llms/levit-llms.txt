# Levit

> Levit Inc. (레브잇) is a software company in Seoul, South Korea, best known for Alwayz
> (올웨이즈) — a mobile-first team-purchase commerce app launched in July 2021 where shoppers
> group together with other buyers to unlock prices below normal retail. Levit also launched
> Ping in March 2021. Levit publishes no public developer API program, API documentation,
> developer portal, or SDKs as of 2026-07-19.

## Company

- [Levit Inc. corporate site](https://www.ilevit.com/): Company overview, products, and contact.
- [Careers (레브잇 채용)](http://team.alwayz.co): Levit hiring site.

## Products

- [Alwayz (올웨이즈)](https://alwayz.co): Team-purchase commerce app — shoppers buy together for lower prices.
- [Alwayz app download](https://alwayzshop.ilevit.com/app): iOS and Android app install links.
- [Alwayz Seller Center](https://alwayzseller.ilevit.com/): Merchant sign-in and seller onboarding for the Alwayz marketplace.
- Ping: Earlier Levit product, launched March 2021.
